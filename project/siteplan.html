Guidebook on Nigeria

Website Project: Discover Nigeria

Site Name

Discover Nigeria

This name was chosen because the website is designed to help visitors learn about Nigeria's rich culture, traditions, food, tourist attractions, fashion, languages, and history. The name clearly communicates the purpose of the site and encourages people to explore everything Nigeria has to offer.

Domain Name: discovernigeria.com

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide educational and engaging information about Nigeria. Visitors will be able to learn about Nigerian culture, traditional foods, fashion, historical landmarks, tourist destinations, and important facts about the country. The website aims to promote appreciation and understanding of Nigeria while serving as a useful resource for students, travelers, and anyone interested in learning more about the nation.

Scenarios

  • What are the most popular tourist attractions to visit in Nigeria?
  • What traditional foods are commonly eaten in different regions of Nigeria?
  • What cultural festivals are celebrated in Nigeria?
  • How has Nigerian fashion evolved over the years?

Color Schema

#008751
#FFFFFF

Green (#008751) will be used for headings, navigation menus, buttons, and accents throughout the website. This color represents Nigeria's national identity and reflects growth, nature, and unity.

White (#FFFFFF) will be used as the primary background color to create a clean and readable design that allows content and images to stand out.

Typography

Heading Font: Georgia

Georgia will be used for headings because it provides a professional and elegant appearance that draws attention to important sections.

Body Font: Arial

Arial will be used for paragraphs and general content because it is simple, modern, and easy to read on both desktop and mobile devices.
