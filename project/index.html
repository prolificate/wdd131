About Nigeria

Nigeria is one of the most vibrant and culturally diverse countries in West Africa. It is known as the "Giant of Africa". Nigeria has over 250 ethnic groups, each with its own traditions, languages,fashion and food. This diversity makes the country rich in culture and creativity. From colorful festivals to traditional music and dances, Nigeria is full of life and expression.
